    SAW output *
    Unit
    Cells when SAW segmented them (cell-bin GEF, or a GEM with CellID), else the DNBs pooled into 8 µm bins. The registered image SAW wrote (_HE_regist.tif or ssDNA) is in the DNBs' coordinates: no registration needed; H&E file below replaces it.
    Space Ranger output *
    Cell outlinesoptional
    Unit
    With cell outlines the cells are the unit: every 2 µm bin is split between the cells it overlaps, by area (each count goes to a cell with the probability of its share; the part outside every cell is left out). Without outlines, or with 8 µm bins chosen, the bins are the unit at 8 µm: 2 µm bins pooled, 8 µm or larger (16 µm, Visium spots) as they are. Space Ranger's alignment places everything on the image: no registration needed.

    Cells table (CSV)
      
    One row per cell: cell id, region, cell type, lineage, type levels, confidence, expression label,
        marker cluster, hand-labeled, niche, Leiden cluster, UMAP 1 and 2 (when computed) and the coordinates (um).
    
      
    
    
    Analysis notebook (Jupyter)
      
    The code of every step with this annotation's parameters (data, placement on the image, annotation
        with its regions, priors and corrections, PCA / UMAP / Leiden, niches) and its results (tables, figures).
        It runs without this app, with the data files and common Python packages, and checks that the labels,
        clusters and niches come out the same.

    Regions (GeoJSON)
      
    The outlines of this version's regions in the image's full-resolution pixels, named by their region, as
        QuPath writes its annotations (QuPath opens it). Import it with the data registered to the same image, or
        use it in other tools. Cells moved into a region without an outline are not in it.
