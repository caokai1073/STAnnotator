## STAnnotator 0.2.60

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

- **Refine cell types**: while a round is being summed up, the agent reviews it, or the sample is annotated again
  after **Accept**, a window in the middle of the screen says what runs and keeps the rest of the app from being used
  until it ends; a message then says it is done. Before, the app could be used meanwhile, and a change made then (e.g.
  regions imported from a GeoJSON while the agent reviewed) started a new round without the agent's review. After 10
  minutes without a proposal the window offers **Stop waiting** (look at the Claude Code window first); the
  annotation after Accept can be stopped from it.
