## STAnnotator 0.2.60

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

- **Refine cell types**: while a round is being summed up, the agent reviews it, or the sample is annotated again
  after **Accept**, a window in the middle of the screen says what runs and keeps the rest of the app from being used
  until it ends; a message then says it is done. Before, the app could be used meanwhile, and a change made then (e.g.
  regions imported from a GeoJSON while the agent reviewed) started a new round without the agent's review. After 10
  minutes without a proposal the window offers **Stop waiting** (look at the Claude Code window first); the
  annotation after Accept can be stopped from it.
- **Exports list the cell types without cells**: next to `cells.csv`, `types_not_found.csv` names every type of the
  marker list that got no cell, why, and how often its markers are detected (also in `CHANGELOG.md` and in the
  results of `stagent batch`). Since 0.2.57 such a type gets no cells instead of a few scattered ones; a script that
  read only the cell table did not see that.
- **Tissue priors by species**: the priors that hold for human or mouse only (e.g. the mouse islet core and mantle,
  the human proximal-colon Paneth cells, the mouse forestomach, depth limits for human tissue) are marked in the
  app's priors. The agent and the Prior window get the sample's species' version, told from its gene names; for an
  H&E only, all with the marks.

### Fixes

- Compare, Signaling, Trajectory and Co-localization show the current niches after the niches are found again or
  renamed, and the Leiden clusters after a UMAP, without reopening the annotation.
- Import: a file in the Cells field that is not a cell table (e.g. `cells.zarr.zip`, the cell outlines) is named as
  such; a sample name with spaces is refused before the import starts, with a name to use instead.
- The marker list check says "127 marker genes of the panel's 480" instead of "127 genes".
- The Mac app no longer builds matplotlib's font cache again at every start.
- Niches with **All slices together** and **Agent chooses**: the agent now finds the niches of all slices together
  (one k for every slice) instead of those of the open slice only, which left the other slices without niches. With
  a slice not annotated yet, the app says at once which slices need an annotation (both ways of choosing k), instead
  of a short error naming the slice by its internal id after the run started.
- Views that kept old results after a change now follow it:
  - Compare, Signaling, Trajectory and Co-localization show niche names given by the agent or accepted from the
    Review panel (the groupings and results kept the old names).
  - The UMAP window recolors after a correction, a prior, Refine or a niche run; Leiden-cluster colors on the map
    follow a new PCA + UMAP; trajectory colors follow a new run, also one the agent made (it now appears as a color
    of the map without reopening the annotation).
  - A report the agent writes appears in the open Report window also when the Review panel is closed.
  - Export and the Data card show a registration correction made from the Review panel.
  - A legend choice of a region that was renamed or deleted no longer hides every cell.
  - An analysis that ends after another annotation was opened is kept for its own annotation instead of being shown
    over the new one; a dot plot of an older result by niche or Leiden cluster asks to run it again once those were
    computed again.
  - Alignment candidates and H&E classes found on an image are refused after another image was imported (search
    again), instead of placing the cells on the old image.
