## STAnnotator 0.2.60

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

- **Refine cell types**: while a round is summed up, the agent reviews it, or the sample is annotated again after
  **Accept**, a window in the middle of the screen says what runs and keeps the rest of the app from being used until
  it ends, so a change made meanwhile no longer starts a round without the agent's review. After 10 minutes without a
  proposal it offers **Stop waiting**.
- **Annotation: a cell type that is most of its level is found**: when its markers are in most cells of the level
  above (e.g. ileum enterocytes among the absorptive cells), its cells went to a sibling type. On an ileum section
  with an IBD list: enterocytes 0 -> 40,862 cells, agreement with the authors 83.9% -> 85.8%.
- **Built-in brain list**: from cortex classes only to 35 cell types (cortical layer types, hippocampal, thalamic,
  cerebellar, striatal and monoaminergic neurons, the oligodendrocyte lineage, ependymal and choroid plexus cells).
  On Allen Brain Cell atlas MERFISH sections, agreement with the atlas rose from 15% to 95%.
- **Tissue priors by species**: priors that hold for human or mouse only are marked; the agent and the Prior window
  get the sample's species' version, told from its gene names.
- **Exports list the cell types without cells**: `types_not_found.csv` names every type of the marker list that got
  no cell and why (also in `stagent batch` results).
- **Niches of all slices together** with **Agent chooses**: the agent now finds one set of niches for all slices
  (one k for every slice), so slices can be compared; before, it found the open slice's only.
- **Steps**: **Analyze** is a step to do and is checked when every analysis is done; **Regions** has an **Import**
  button for GeoJSON outlines (e.g. from QuPath).

### Fixes

- Fixed some bugs and made small improvements, among them views that kept old results after niches, UMAP or names
  changed, clearer messages for import and analysis errors, faster start of the Mac app, and more reliable file
  writing on Windows.
