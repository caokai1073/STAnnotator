## STAnnotator 0.2.49

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, cell-cell
communication and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### What's new

**Screenshots in bug reports**
- **Report a bug** (the bug icon at the top right) now takes up to 3 screenshots: click **Add screenshot…**, or drop
  or paste them into the dialog. They show as thumbnails you can remove before sending.
- Uploaded reports arrive with each screenshot as a picture of its own next to the report, so a report with pictures
  can be much larger than before. **Save to file…** keeps them inside the report.
- A report still holds no expression data or cell tables, and no images but the screenshots you add.

**Registration check: local corrections are checked before they are offered**
- A local correction is now applied to the cells and measured again before it is offered, instead of being predicted.
  Readings that stay off even after the cells are moved onto the nuclei (usually a repeating structure matched one
  period off) are left out and outlined dotted in the figure, so a correction no longer says "fixed" when it is not.

**A new interactive tutorial**
- The website's tutorial is now interactive: it plays the app step by step, with chapters you can jump to.
