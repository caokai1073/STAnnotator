## STAnnotator 0.2.50

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### Important fix: gene names with a space

- Gene names holding a space, such as CosMx's **"MHC I"**, were read as two names, so every gene after it in the
  panel read the next gene's counts (on a 1,000-plex CosMx panel, the genes from M to Z). Gene names are now read
  exactly as imported.
- **If a sample of yours has such a gene name** (most CosMx 1,000-plex panels), annotate it again and run its
  analyses again after updating: annotations and analyses made before used the shifted names. The imported data
  itself is right; it does not need to be imported again.

### Also

- A bug report's README lists only what the report holds, and, when the app worked on a server, says what was added
  on your own computer (local/).
