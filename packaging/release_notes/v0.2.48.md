## STAnnotator 0.2.48

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, cell-cell
communication and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### What's new

**A tidier toolbar**
- **Report a bug** is the bug icon at the top right, between **?** and **›**.
- **Local** (or **Remote** while you work on a server) sits beside **Import** and **Export**.

**Help: a page on the agent**
- **? → Agent** shows what you can ask the agent, with example requests, what it does and what comes back: bringing
  data in, aligning the H&E, marker lists, priors and corrections, regions, analyses, review and reports.

**Built-in marker lists, checked and updated**
- Every organ's list was checked against the Human Protein Atlas and organ atlases, and the intestinal ones against
  published annotations. Wrong or unspecific markers are fixed (for example mast cells, monocytes, liver lymphatic
  endothelium, breast basal cells, pancreatic exocrine cells), current gene symbols are added (LORICRIN, IRAG2,
  CIMAP3), and missing cell types are added (lung neuroendocrine cells, ionocytes and capillary endothelium, kidney
  glomerular endothelium and JG cells, heart endocardium, skin hair follicle and Schwann cells, and others).
- Panels without the classic genes keep more cell types: the lists now carry alternative markers for epithelium,
  smooth muscle, tuft cells, enteric neurons and enteroendocrine cells.

**Bug reports say who sent them**
- A report's file name carries a random id of your installation and, if you give it, your name or email, so the
  developers can tell reports apart and reply.

**Fixes**
- After opening the app from the agent's link, **Refresh** and **Load…** stay on the annotation you are working on.
- A region keeps the color you picked when you undo its renaming.
- An annotation loaded from a `.stannot` file is marked "loaded copy" in the list.
