## STAnnotator 0.2.64

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### Fixes

- A section aligned automatically whose cells sit far off their nuclei in places (a distorted or folded section) was told to **Align again** over and over, though aligning again gives the same placement: the check now offers the local registration (which handles large shifts) for it, and the agent or a registration file when that cannot help.
- On Windows, opening STAnnotator while an update was still being installed failed with no window (the installer was
  still replacing its files): the app now says that the update is being installed and opens by itself when it is done;
  an installation left incomplete says so, with the link to the installer, instead of failing silently.
