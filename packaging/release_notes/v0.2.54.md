## STAnnotator 0.2.54

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

**Faster on large data**
- On a Mac, the exact neighbor search runs on the Apple GPU (with the same result as on the processor).
- PCA is about 3 times faster (a 190,000-cell, 5,000-gene section: 29 s to 10 s), on every computer and in every
  analysis that uses it (annotation, PCA + UMAP).
- CosMx and MERSCOPE counts import about 7 times faster (a 600,000-cell CosMx 6K file: 110 s to 15 s).

**Check registration, by the app**
- **Steps → Check registration** now measures the cells against the nuclei over the whole tissue itself (about a
  minute), without asking the agent, and shows the result in a window: the histogram of the deviation and where on
  the tissue the cells sit off their nuclei.
- Where the tissue shifted, **Do local registration** moves the cells onto their nuclei. They are measured again and
  a second window shows the result: **Accept** keeps it, **Undo** puts the cells back.
- An alignment whose check found the cells on their nuclei needs no further check once accepted.

**Fixes**
- **Remove all** now frees the memory the samples used.

**The same results**
- PCA is now computed exactly, the same way on every computer: an annotation made with 0.2.53 and run again can
  differ in a very small share of cells (in our tests the neighbors of 99.9% of the cells stayed the same).

### If you are updating from 0.2.52 or earlier

**0.2.53** made annotations the same every time (they could differ when run twice in the app), the exact neighbor
search 30 times faster and the default, Filter cells and genes 3 to 4 times faster, kept an analysis's progress
while the agent works, and showed the agent's alignment step by step.

**0.2.52** brought the Steps panel, QuPath-like region drawing, a check of every alignment before you accept it, the
organ's literature priors in the agent's Review (**Prior**), Manual / Auto, the agent's model choice, DESeq2 and the
project notebook.
