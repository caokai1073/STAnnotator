## STAnnotator 0.2.51

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### What's new

**Updates while the app stays open**
- The app now also looks for a new release every 6 hours while it is open, not only when it starts.
- It offers a release by itself at most once a day: **Later** waits until the next day, **Skip this version** until
  the next release. **Check for updates** in About always shows it. Without an internet connection nothing is shown.
- The update window shows what every release since yours changed, newest first, not only the latest one.

**A daily count of installations**
- Once a day the desktop app sends us a count: a random installation id, the version and the system (macOS or
  Windows), nothing else. It tells us how many installations are in use while we test STAnnotator.
- **? → About → Daily count** shows the last count and turns it off.

### If you are updating from an earlier version

**0.2.50: gene names with a space (important)**
- Gene names holding a space, such as CosMx's **"MHC I"**, were read as two names, so every gene after it in the
  panel read the next gene's counts (on a 1,000-plex CosMx panel, the genes from M to Z). Gene names are now read
  exactly as imported.
- **If a sample of yours has such a gene name** (most CosMx 1,000-plex panels), annotate it again and run its
  analyses again after updating: annotations and analyses made before used the shifted names. The imported data
  itself is right; it does not need to be imported again.
- A bug report's README lists only what the report holds, and, when the app worked on a server, says what was added
  on your own computer (local/).

**0.2.49: screenshots in bug reports, checked registration corrections**
- **Report a bug** takes up to 3 screenshots: click **Add screenshot…**, or drop or paste them into the dialog.
- A local registration correction is applied to the cells and measured again before it is offered; readings that
  stay off are left out and outlined dotted in the figure.

**0.2.48: toolbar, agent help, marker lists, usage sharing**
- **Report a bug** is the bug icon at the top right; **Local** / **Remote** sits beside **Import** and **Export**.
- **? → Agent** shows what you can ask the agent, with example requests.
- The built-in marker lists were checked against the Human Protein Atlas and organ atlases: wrong or unspecific
  markers fixed, current gene symbols and missing cell types added.
- The app asks once whether it may send the developers a report of its use every 3 days (never expression data,
  cell tables or images); **? → About → Usage sharing** turns it off or on.
