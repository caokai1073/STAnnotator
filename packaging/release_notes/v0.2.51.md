## STAnnotator 0.2.51

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### What's new

**Updates while the app stays open**
- The app now also looks for a new release every 6 hours while it is open, not only when it starts.
- It offers a release by itself at most once a day: **Later** waits until the next day, **Skip this version** until
  the next release. **Check for updates** in About always shows it. Without an internet connection nothing is shown.
- The update window shows what every release since yours changed, newest first, not only the latest one.

**A daily count of installations**
- Once a day the desktop app sends us a count: a random installation id, the version and the system (macOS or
  Windows), nothing else. It tells us how many installations are in use while we test STAnnotator.
- **? → About → Daily count** shows the last count and turns it off.
