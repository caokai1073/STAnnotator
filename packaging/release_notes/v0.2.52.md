## STAnnotator 0.2.52

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### What's new

**Steps: where the sample stands**
- A **Steps** panel on the map shows the usual flow (import, check registration, filter, annotate, regions and priors,
  agent review, analyze), what is done, and a button to each next step. **Analyze** unfolds into Embed, Niche,
  Compare, Signal and Trajectory.
- The **Regions** and **Cell types** panels stay on the map while you switch slices or annotations.

**Drawing regions, as in QuPath**
- Tools: Move (M), Rectangle (R), Ellipse (O), Polygon (P), Pen (F, freehand) and Brush (B); Alt erases. Click a tool
  again, or press Esc, to put it down.
- A plain click on the map shows the cell; Alt / Ctrl / Cmd + click selects a region (Shift adds).
- A whole region can be deleted from its row. GeoJSON import (e.g. from QuPath) is much faster and turns pixel
  outlines into smooth curves.

**Registration**
- **Align with Agent** lets you choose the samples to align, all at once if you like.
- Before you accept an alignment, the whole tissue is checked: the window shows how far the cells sit from their
  nuclei. When some areas are off, choose **Do local registration** (the alignment, then the cells moved onto their
  nuclei there, at once) or **Align anyway**. In **Auto** mode the local registration is done by itself.
- Export (registration, cell positions) is available as soon as the cells sit on the image.

**The agent**
- **Manual / Auto**: in Auto the agent's changes are applied as they arrive, without the review window (Undo takes
  one back).
- **Prior** (beside Review): the organ, chosen from your annotation, and its literature priors. Remove the ones that
  do not hold for your tissue, or add your own in plain words: the agent writes their rules. The agent's Review
  checks your annotation and regions against them.
- A box next to **Agent** chooses the agent's model (Fable, Opus, Sonnet, Haiku or Claude Code's default).
- For slices annotated together, the agent names niches and adds a prior in every slice at once, with one Accept.
- Analyses still running when you reconnect or reload are followed again (progress, Stop and the result).

**Samples, data and analyses**
- Replace or remove one sample (Import: Replace the existing sample; Remove beside Rename). Annotations can be
  renamed too.
- **Remove all** also closes the agent's window.
- **Filter cells and genes** shows its progress.
- **Compare**: DESeq2 on pseudobulk counts per slice or patient, for a project's slices.
- CosMx / MERSCOPE files named .csv but compressed (as AtoMx can export them) now import.
- Much less memory for large data (CosMx 6K, Visium HD, Stereo-seq), with the same results.
- Switching samples is faster.

**Notebooks**
- **Save project…** writes one notebook that replays a project annotated with all slices together.
- Choose which annotation a notebook replays. A notebook installs its Python packages in its first cell.

**Reproducible annotations**
- An annotation now comes out the same on every computer: the neighbor search runs the same way everywhere and its
  packages are pinned. An annotation made with an earlier version and run again may differ in a small share of cells.

**Fixes**
- Many fixes from a review of the whole code before this release. Among them: the app could close when an analysis
  started while it was getting ready after launch; a failed or stopped Replace import now always puts the old sample
  back; thresholds that remove every cell no longer move the annotations to the trash.

### If you are updating from 0.2.50 or earlier

**0.2.50: gene names with a space (important)**
- Gene names holding a space, such as CosMx's **"MHC I"**, were read as two names, so every gene after it in the
  panel read the next gene's counts. **If a sample of yours has such a gene name** (most CosMx 1,000-plex panels),
  annotate it again and run its analyses again: annotations and analyses made before 0.2.50 used the shifted names.

**0.2.51**: the app looks for updates while it is open, at most once a day; a daily count of installations (turn it
off in ? → About).
