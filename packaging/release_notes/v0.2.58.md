## STAnnotator 0.2.58

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

**Fixes to 0.2.57**
- Cell types whose positive marker is one OR group (e.g. `Endothelial: PECAM1|VWF|CDH5|CLDN5|EMCN+`) are found again
  by the rare-type search: the genes of an OR group now count one by one. In 0.2.57 such types (often lineages:
  Endothelial, Myeloid, Epithelial...) could be missed, and their cells labeled as a neighboring type (endothelial
  cells as smooth muscle on a CosMx colon section). A raised gene in at least half of a type's markers is enough
  again; only a type whose markers name a single gene (e.g. `ICC: Kit+`) is still looked for in single cells only.
- A cell type not found because its parent type has no cells now says why the parent was not found (under the
  annotation, **Not found**).
- The memory estimate shown before a run is right on a Mac (it underestimated there by about 20%).

If you installed 0.2.57 and annotated with a marker list that has OR groups, annotate again with 0.2.58.
