## STAnnotator 0.2.63

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

- **Groups of slices and of regions**: **Group** (Data tab, beside Rename) puts the slices of a project into groups,
  e.g. condition: UC or control, and **Group** in the Regions panel puts regions into groups, e.g. mucosa_1 and
  mucosa_2 in mucosa. Compare then compares the groups (DE genes, composition, signaling, and for slices DESeq2 with
  the slices of a group as replicates).
- **Image brightness and contrast**: **Adjust** (beside the **Image** box on the map) sets the brightness and contrast
  of the image shown, and for a fluorescence image (DAPI, a protein / IF channel) its display range and gamma; each
  image and channel keeps its own settings.
- **Images of several channels, and managing images**: a fluorescence file of several channels (e.g. an OME-TIFF of
  DAPI, CD45 and PanCK) gives one entry per channel in the **Image** box. **Discard** (Steps → Image) takes images out
  of a sample, and **Locate…** finds an image again after its file was moved or renamed, the cells on it as before.
- **Reproduction guide for agents**: **Export** also saves a zip for an AI agent (or a person) who repeats the analysis
  without the app: a step-by-step guide (input files with checksums, filter, placement, annotation, embedding,
  niches, every analysis you ran, how the figures are drawn), links to the code of this version, the results to
  check against, the data tables and colors, and optionally the notebook.
- **Screenshot view**: the camera button at the map's bottom right (or the key **s**) hides every box and panel for a
  clean picture of the map (the legend too, unless you tick it); **Esc** brings everything back.
- **Genes and programs in the project overview**: the overview of a project colors every slice by a gene or a gene
  program, on one color scale for all slices.

### Improvements

- **Built-in marker lists checked on labeled atlases, human and mouse**: the lists were tested on labeled single-cell
  atlases of each organ and on mouse data, and changed where it helped. Brain: CA1 and CA3 pyramidal neurons and more
  markers (Allen ABC MERFISH). Kidney: thin limbs and principal cells told apart better (79% instead of 70% of the
  cells agree with the KPMP atlas). Lung, breast, lymph node / tonsil, stomach, liver, colon and small intestine:
  e.g. club cells, luminal progenitors, neck mucous and enteroendocrine cells, hepatic stellate cells, Tregs and a
  monocyte type. Mouse gene names where the human ones do not exist in mouse, and alternatives for panels without
  the usual genes.
- **Genes and programs on a color bar**: Color by a gene or a program shows each cell's value on a continuous color
  bar, on one scale for all slices of a project, the cells where it is not detected drawn under the others.
- **The instrument's DAPI checked quickly**: the instrument's own DAPI, put under the cells by their coordinates, is
  looked at by itself in seconds (the cells against its nuclei in 20 windows), so a DAPI of another run or the wrong
  file is caught; **Check** runs the full check. A fluorescence image is no longer aligned as an H&E, and an image of
  the instrument's images' size (another channel or stain of the same scan) is placed without a registration.
- **Better alignment when things are off**: when the cells sit far off their nuclei over most of the tissue, the check
  advises **Align again** (one click, with Undo) instead of the local registration; aligning also tries other pixel
  sizes when the one in the image file is wrong.
- Small improvements: in each Refine round the agent looks at the tissue image before proposing; on a narrow map the
  agent's buttons and Adjust show as icons so the boxes stay on one row; the DE dot plot names every group and keeps
  the group names in sight when it scrolls sideways; images with the same name are told apart by their file; the Data
  card says when the image is missing; Annotate and Filter wait while an alignment or a registration check runs.

### Fixes

- Importing a Xenium output by choosing its files left out its DAPI, and choosing the instrument's own DAPI afterwards
  ran a full alignment search; the DAPI is now found and placed at once. A Xenium output with all four morphology
  images now shows each of them.
- On large gene panels (some 5,000 genes) the agent's marker check and gene list were too long for Claude Code; they
  are now about half as long with the same numbers.
- Niches computed twice on the same data could differ slightly, and niches of one slice computed after those of all
  slices used the project's programs, so an exported notebook did not always reproduce them; both are fixed (niches
  of an earlier version may come out somewhat different when computed again).
- Files exported from a renamed slice or sample still carried the name it was imported under; an exported notebook
  could stop in its annotation cell.
- Fixed some bugs, among them composition by a region grouping over all slices, a confusing error when a Refine round
  was stopped while the agent worked, the image step shown as done after a check that advised aligning again, and the
  orange **Update** button turning all white under the mouse.
