## STAnnotator 0.2.63

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

### Improvements

- When the cells sit far off their nuclei over most of the tissue (a rotation or scale error of the whole alignment), the registration check and the agent's review now advise **aligning again** instead of the local registration, with one click (Align automatically, with Undo).
- Aligning an image without a registration file also tries other pixel sizes when the one stored in the image file fits badly (a wrong size in the file no longer stops it); the agent can be given the image's pixel size.

### Fixes

- An exported notebook stopped in its annotation cell ("module 'sa.config' has no attribute 'read_settings'"); it now runs through.
- Fixed some bugs, among them the orange **Update** button turning all white under the mouse.
