## STAnnotator 0.2.53

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

### What's new

**The same annotation every time (important)**
- Annotating the same data twice in the app could give different clusters and labels (up to about 10% of the
  cells): from the second annotation after the app started, the clustering step was not seeded. It is now, so an
  annotation, PCA + UMAP or niches give the same result every time, on every computer, and the same as an exported
  notebook. This was also in 0.2.52 and earlier.
- **If you compare annotations**, annotate again with 0.2.53: annotations made with 0.2.51 or earlier differ from
  0.2.53's in a sizable share of cells (about 16% in our tests), those made with 0.2.52 less.

**Exact neighbors: fast, and the default**
- The exact neighbor search is about 30 times faster on the computer's processor and needs under 1 GB of memory: a
  5,000-gene Xenium section of 190,000 cells in about 9 seconds, 400,000 cells in under a minute on 8 cores (it used
  to take minutes and, for large samples, more memory than a laptop has).
- **Exact** is now the default: the true neighbors, the same on every computer. **Approximate** stays for very
  large samples; the time estimate suggests it when Exact would take over 10 minutes.

**Working with the agent**
- An analysis keeps its progress bar and Stop while the agent opens another sample; when it ends, a message says
  where its result is.
- While the agent aligns images, the bottom of the window shows which sample it is on and what it is doing, until
  every chosen sample is aligned.

**Faster**
- **Filter cells and genes** is 3 to 4 times faster (two slices of 190,000 cells: 34 s to 8 s), and imports save
  their data faster too.

**Fixes**
- CosMx and MERSCOPE files ending in **.csv.gz** can be chosen in the Import dialog on macOS (they were grayed out).
- A new version is offered when the app starts, also when it was checked a few hours before; a version offered
  earlier the same day no longer hides a newer one.
- The notebook of one slice of a project annotated together says why its labels can differ from the app's.

### If you are updating from 0.2.51 or earlier

**0.2.52** brought the Steps panel, QuPath-like region drawing, a check of every alignment before you accept it with
one-click local registration, the organ's literature priors in the agent's Review (**Prior**), Manual / Auto, the
agent's model choice, DESeq2, the project notebook, and many fixes. See its release notes.

**0.2.50: gene names with a space (important)**: gene names such as CosMx's **"MHC I"** were read as two names, so
the genes after it read the next gene's counts. If a sample of yours has such a gene name, annotate it again after
updating.
