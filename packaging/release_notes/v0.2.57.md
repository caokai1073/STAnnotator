## STAnnotator 0.2.57

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

**Refine the annotation with the agent, round by round**
- After a marker annotation the **Refine** window opens (on by default in the Type tab; **Refine cell types** opens it
  later). The app sums up what may be wrong: types whose markers are not raised in their cells, markers that do not
  tell a type from its siblings, types not found, and the changes since the round before.
- The organ is inferred from the cell types and the sample's name, or asked. The agent reviews the whole annotation
  with that organ's priors and proposes edits of the marker list one by one, each with its reason and evidence.
- Write to the agent at any time: your words come first. Tick the edits you want and **Accept**: the app annotates
  again with them, keeping your regions, priors and corrections, and the next round opens.

**Every cell gets a cell type**
- Cells the marker step could not type (often cells with few transcripts) now get a cell type from their marker
  genes instead of Unassigned: the type whose cells most often show the same marker genes detected and not detected,
  weighted by its share of cells, among the types the marker step found; their confidence says how sure. Only cells
  with none of the list's marker genes detected stay Unassigned. On a mouse colon slice with the authors' labels,
  agreement rose from 60% to 67% (cell types) and from 66% to 78% (lineages); Unassigned fell from 22% to 0.5%.

**Annotation uses where the cells are**
- The cells the marker step could not type are now also typed with their neighbors: their marker genes first, then,
  round by round, the cell types around them, until the labels settle. The cells the marker step typed keep their
  own expression. On the mouse colon slice, cell-type agreement rose to 68% and lineage agreement to 80%; on three
  human colon Xenium sections the result is unchanged.
- **Leave out spillover** (Type tab, on by default): transcripts a cell's outline caught from its neighbors are not
  counted as its own (a gene counts only above a tenth of its neighbors' mean). On the mouse colon slice lineage
  agreement rose to 83% and 2,600 more cells were typed by their markers; on the Xenium sections it changed little.
  Turn it off to count every transcript in a cell's outline, as before.

**Marker annotation finds the rare cell types of your list**
- A cell type of the marker list that no cluster went to is now looked for: in finer clusters of the same cells,
  and, where its cells are too few or too scattered to form a cluster (Paneth cells among stem cells, ICC between
  muscle cells), in the single cells that clearly show its markers. Each is taken only where the cells really show the
  type's markers. On a 270,000-cell colon section this found BEST4+ colonocytes (821 cells, before 7), monocytes
  (1,066, before 5), effector/memory CD4 T cells (629, before 4), pDC (109), Paneth-like cells (36) and others.
- A type still without cells is listed under the annotation (Type tab: **Not found**), with how often its markers
  are detected, instead of getting a handful of cells by chance.
- Annotations made now differ from those of 0.2.56 and earlier for the same data and marker list; annotations you
  made before keep their labels.

- **Niche → Co-localization**: which groups of cells (cell types, lineages, regions, niches…) sit next to each other
  more or less often than by chance, from the neighbor pairs within a radius against labels shuffled within each
  slice (seeded: the same numbers every run). A table of z-scores and of observed / expected, the strongest pairs
  together and apart, Save CSV, and Ask Agent to interpret it; the agent has it as a tool too.
- **Markers… → Refine with the agent** refines the list in the editor (imported or your own), keeping your cell
  types, names and structure, and saves it as `<name>_refined`; the organ is optional then. With the editor empty
  it still completes the organ's built-in list.
- The **Annotate** tab is now called **Type**; **Refine cell types** under its Annotate button opens the Refine window.
- **All slices together** is now a switch in each of the Type, Embed and Niche tabs, for that analysis.
- **Compare** runs in the background with its progress, and can be stopped (**Stop** under its button), like the
  other analyses.
- **Correct locally** in Review → Spatial organization shows the local registration's progress, then its result to
  keep or undo, as Steps → Check registration does.
- **Regions → Import** of a GeoJSON already imported (the same coordinates) says so and changes nothing; of a file
  with some new outlines, only those are added.
- **Import** opens empty again after an import (the files and choices of the last one are cleared).
- Messages appear in the middle of the screen.
- **PCA + UMAP → Select**: the count of the selected cells is shown below the plot.
