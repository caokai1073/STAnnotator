## STAnnotator 0.2.57

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

- **Compare** runs in the background with its progress, and can be stopped (**Stop** under its button), like the
  other analyses.
- **Correct locally** in Review → Spatial organization shows the local registration's progress, then its result to
  keep or undo, as Steps → Check registration does.
- **Regions → Import** of a GeoJSON already imported (the same coordinates) says so and changes nothing; of a file
  with some new outlines, only those are added.
- **Import** opens empty again after an import (the files and choices of the last one are cleared).
- Messages appear in the middle of the screen.
- **PCA + UMAP → Select**: the count of the selected cells is on its own line, below the buttons.
