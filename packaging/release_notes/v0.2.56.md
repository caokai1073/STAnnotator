## STAnnotator 0.2.56

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

**Faster analyses, with the same results**
Every result is the same as before, number for number; only the time changes. On a 270,000-cell, 5,000-gene section:
- **DE genes of every group** (Compare, group "all") and **top genes per group**: about 6 times faster (49 s to 8 s).
- **DE genes** of one group: about 2.5 times faster (4.5 s to 1.7 s).
- **Cell-cell communication**: about 3 times faster (34 s to 12 s).
- **Niches** of another annotation of the same sample: its gene programs are computed once per sample (11 s to 1 s).
- **PCA + UMAP** and **trajectories** (pseudotime) reuse the PCA and neighbors already computed for the same cells:
  a pseudotime of all cells right after PCA + UMAP takes 7 s instead of 20 s.

**Cell positions as the registration of an import**
- The cell positions saved by the app (Export → Registration → Save cell positions) can be given as the registration
  file of an import of the same cells: every cell goes back where it was, local corrections and moved pieces included.
  Cells the file does not list are placed by the affine that fits the file best.
