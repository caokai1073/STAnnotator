## STAnnotator 0.2.56

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's fixed

**H&E images with JPEG tiles open and import again**
- The last 0.2.55 installers (build 1db3fbb, shown under Help → About) could not read or write JPEG-compressed
  images: importing an H&E stopped with "DelayedImportError: could not import name 'jpeg_encode' from 'imagecodecs'",
  and H&E images stored with JPEG tiles (most whole-slide OME-TIFF and SVS files) could not be shown. A new version of
  an image library, released the day they were built, had renamed the part the app uses. The app now uses the earlier version, and every build checks that it
  reads and writes each image compression of H&E files before it is published.
- When an import fails, its error is also written to the app's log, so a **Report bug** includes it.

**Slices with the same genes join a project, in any order**
- CosMx exports (and Xenium ones) can list the same genes in another order; such slices were refused by **Projects**
  ("must share one gene panel") although their panel is the same. A slice with the project's genes in another order
  now joins: its genes are put in the project's order once (the same counts, gene for gene; a large slice takes up to
  a minute). A slice that has annotations keeps its order, and the other slices take it.
- When gene names really differ, the message says how many, and which ones when there are fewer than 20.

**Cell positions as the registration: two fixes**
- A saved annotation (**Save annotation…**, .stannot) of a sample registered by a cell positions file now carries
  that file. Loaded on the same data in another data folder, the cells a filter had left out come back where the file
  puts them (**Restore all**, the notebook), no longer on the fitted affine (up to 20 px off). Such an annotation file
  needs 0.2.56 or later to load; other annotation files still load in 0.2.55.
- **Undo correction** of a registration by cell positions stays undone when the image is added again.

**Other changes**
- The daily count of installations (Help → About; only a random id, the version and the system) counts days in
  Boston time, and counts an installation again on the day it is updated.
- The installers are built from a fixed list of every library and its version, so a library released on the day of
  a build can no longer change the app.

### If you are updating from 0.2.54 or earlier

**0.2.55** gave the same annotations on Macs with an M5 chip as on every other computer, made DE genes, top genes
per group, cell-cell communication, niches and trajectories 2 to 6 times faster with the same results, and accepted
the cell positions saved by the app as the registration file of an import.

**0.2.54** ran the exact neighbor search on the Apple GPU, made PCA about 3 times faster and the same on every
computer, imported CosMx and MERSCOPE counts about 7 times faster, and let the app check the registration itself
(**Steps → Check registration**) with **Do local registration** where the tissue shifted.
