## STAnnotator 0.2.55

Spatial transcriptomics on the tissue image, from registration and cell types to regions, niches, composition and DE
genes, cell-cell communication, trajectories and comparisons across slices, with an AI agent at your side.

**This version needs macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later on a Mac.**

### What's new

**The same results on every Mac**
- On Macs with an M5 chip, the exact neighbor search on the GPU computed in reduced precision, so an annotation could
  differ from the same annotation on another computer (in our tests in 6 to 9% of the cells). It now computes in full
  precision and gives the same neighbors as every other computer; if a Mac's GPU cannot, the search runs on the
  processor instead.
- An annotation made with 0.2.54 on an M5 Mac and run again gives the new, correct result. Other Macs (M1 to M4),
  Windows and Linux are not affected.

**Check registration**
- **Do local registration** is offered where areas of the tissue sit off their nuclei (as the alignment's own check
  judges it: 5% of the tissue in patches off, or a large shift), no longer for one small spot, and not when the
  correction would bring no area back onto its nuclei (then align the image again).
- **Filter cells and genes** keeps the registration check (filtering moves no cell): Steps no longer asks to check
  again.
- **Undo** of a local registration puts back the check of the placement it returns to.
- The sample line no longer says "image waiting for its alignment" once an alignment is applied.

**Fixes**
- A region proposed by the agent counts the cells inside its outline, the same cells **Accept** adds.
- The **Filter** window says that the drawn regions go to the trash with the annotations, and how to keep them
  (Export → Save regions…, then Import in the Regions panel).
- The notebook of one slice of a project says why its Leiden clusters and niches differ from the app's (they were
  computed on all slices together; Export → Save project replays them exactly).
- The sample's data folder shows where the sample is now, also after **Change data folder**.
- UMAP keeps its current layout with newer versions of its library.

### If you are updating from 0.2.53 or earlier

**0.2.54** ran the exact neighbor search on the Apple GPU, made PCA about 3 times faster and the same on every
computer, imported CosMx and MERSCOPE counts about 7 times faster, and let the app check the registration itself
(**Steps → Check registration**) with **Do local registration** where the tissue shifted.

**0.2.53** made annotations the same every time, the exact neighbor search 30 times faster and the default, and
Filter cells and genes 3 to 4 times faster.
