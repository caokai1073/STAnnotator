STAnnotator · Interactive tutorial

The tutorial is the real app: open it on a computer (a window at least 760 px wide).

Using the tutorial

  • Space play / pause. While paused the app is live: click, zoom the H&E, switch colors and tabs.
  • ← → previous / next step, Shift + ← → previous / next chapter.
  • Click or drag the bar at the bottom, or a chapter on the left, to jump.
  • The left pane replays the agent (Claude Code) as it worked on this sample; the right pane is the real app on the recorded data.
  • Computations (new imports, analyses, the agent itself) need the installed app: the demo answers only what was recorded.
